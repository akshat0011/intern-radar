    Before you upload, read this.

    This site never stores your resume — the text is held only for as long as the request takes, then discarded. But it is sent to Google's Gemini API to do the rewriting, and this site runs on Gemini's free tier, which permits Google to use submitted data to improve their models.

    So: remove your home address, ID numbers, and anything else you would not want processed by a third party. Your name, email, education and experience are all the tailorer actually needs.
